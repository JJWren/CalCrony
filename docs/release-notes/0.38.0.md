### What's new

- **Free slots count CalCrony events.** `/availability slots` no longer lists a time when a member is busy with a CalCrony event in this server: one they created, or one they are going to.
- **If you saved your available hours:** a list of your free slots by name now also leaves out your CalCrony events in the server the list is made in. `/settings available-hours reset` stops lists by name.

### How to use it

1. Run `/availability slots who:@GM` as before. A session the GM has scheduled in CalCrony is now left out of their free time.
2. An event counts for whoever created it, whatever they answer, and for members holding a seat on its attending option ("Going" by default). A waitlisted RSVP or any other answer does not block.
3. A repeating event also blocks its later dates for the member who created it.
