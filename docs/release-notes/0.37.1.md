### What's new

- **Short weekend days in dates.** `sat` and `sun` now read as Saturday and Sunday wherever you type a date in words: `sat 6pm` is the coming Saturday at 6 PM. Before, it came back as today at 6 PM.
- **Deadlines.** `by friday 6pm`, `before sat at noon` and `by end of day friday` now read as the date and time they name. Before, they were not understood.
- **A day and a time written apart.** `fri (6pm)`, `fri. 6pm` and `friday from 6pm` are Friday at 6 PM. Before, they came back as today at 6 PM.
- **A weekday on its own day.** `wed 9am`, typed on a Wednesday after 9 AM, is next Wednesday. Before, it was refused as past.
- **Repeats.** A date such as `every saturday 8pm` is still refused, and the reply now says why and points to the repeat option.

### How to use it

1. Create an event for the weekend: `/create title:"Game night" when:"sat 6pm"`.
2. See when your players are free on Sunday: `/availability slots who:@players when:sun`.
3. Close RSVPs the evening before: `/create title:"Game night" when:"sat 6pm" rsvp-close:"by friday 6pm"`.
4. Make it weekly by typing the first date and setting the repeat: `/create title:"Game night" when:"sat 8pm" repeat:weekly`.
