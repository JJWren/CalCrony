### What's new

- **Dates up to ten years ahead.** A date typed in words, such as `in 3 days` or `june 12 6pm`, can now be at most ten years away. A later date is turned down with a message that says so.
- **One mention per scheduled ping.** A scheduled ping notifies the role or member picked in the `mention` option of `/notify`, and nobody else. A mention typed into a title, a message or a reminder is shown as text.
- **Scheduled pings follow the event.** `/notify` is for the event's creator or a server manager, like editing the event.

### How to use it

1. Ping a group an hour before an event: `/notify event:"Game night" minutes-before:60 mention:@players`.
